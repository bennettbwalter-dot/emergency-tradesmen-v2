About Us

Roofing, Women, Breaking the Barriers

Roofing4Women was founded on a simple belief: exceptional roofing and a more inclusive trade can go hand in hand.

Our Story

Founded by Amelie Rose, Roofing4Women set out to challenge expectations in a traditionally male-dominated industry. What started as a personal mission grew into a trusted roofing business serving homeowners and businesses alike.

Every roof we install or repair is a statement: that quality, care, and craftsmanship come in every form. We're proud to lead by example and inspire the next generation of tradespeople.

  • 🎯
    Our MissionDeliver outstanding roofing while breaking down barriers in the trade.
  • 💙
    Our ValuesIntegrity, quality, and genuine care for every client and project.
  • 🌟
    Our PromiseReliable work, fair pricing, and a roof you can trust for years.

Why It Matters

We're more than a roofing company — we're proof that skill and dedication know no boundaries. By championing women in the trade, we're building stronger roofs and a stronger, more inclusive industry.
