# UK/US Localization Playbook for Trade Blogs

## Domain Rules

- UK/global content -> `https://emergencytradesmen.net`
- US content -> `https://emergencycontractors.net`

## Terminology Mapping

| UK wording | US wording |
|---|---|
| tradesmen / tradesperson | contractor |
| boiler | furnace / water heater (context dependent) |
| central heating | HVAC / heating system |
| postcode | ZIP code |
| emergency call-out | emergency service call |
| fuse box / consumer unit | electrical panel / breaker panel |

## Spelling and Style

- UK: optimise, colour, organisation, metre
- US: optimize, color, organization, meter

Keep spelling consistent with domain intent.

## Regulatory Cues (Examples)

### UK

- Gas Safe Register
- NICEIC
- Part P (electrical safety)
- Building Regulations and Water Supply regulations

### US

- NEC (National Electrical Code)
- NFPA standards
- EPA Section 608 (HVAC refrigerants)
- State-level contractor licensing boards

Use regulation references when they materially support safety/compliance advice.

## Intent Signals by Market

### UK

- Include local phrasing around call-outs, council/building standards, and domestic trade norms.

### US

- Include state/municipal licensing context, permit language, and homeowner insurance implications where relevant.

## QA Checklist

1. Does the post use the right domain and canonical?
2. Is terminology market-specific and consistent?
3. Are safety/legal references appropriate to country intent?
4. Are currency and units localized?
5. Are CTAs aligned to target market service pages?
