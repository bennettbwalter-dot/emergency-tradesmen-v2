**Your heating is off - so why is the boiler pressure still dropping? ☀️**

A combi boiler that repeatedly loses pressure is telling you something. Before you call for help:

✅ Check the gauge when the system is cool  
✅ Look around radiators, valves and visible pipework for moisture  
✅ Check the outside pressure-relief pipe for dripping  
❌ Do not keep topping it up and ignore the cause  
❌ Never remove the boiler casing

Our guide explains the safe checks and when it is time for a Gas Safe registered engineer.
