## Technical Requirements: Electrical Panel Upgrades and Capacity Issues

Many EV charger failures occur because the home's electrical panel cannot sustain the continuous load required for modern fast charging. If your lights flicker or your main breaker trips while charging, your system likely needs an upgrade. 

Upgrading your electrical panel is a proactive way to prevent emergency calls in the future. A qualified electrician can assess your total home load and install a higher capacity breaker or a dedicated circuit for your EVSE.

## Troubleshooting Connectivity and Software Related Charging Errors

Internet and app connectivity issues account for nearly one-third of all reported home charger problems in 2026. Before requesting an emergency call-out, verify that your Wi-Fi connection is stable and the manufacturer's app is updated. 

A "smart" charger that cannot communicate with the server may enter a fail-safe mode and stop charging. Resetting your router or the charger's communication module can often resolve these non-electrical faults without a technician. If a software reset does not restore functionality, the internal communication board may have failed. At this stage, a professional repair is required to replace the logic board and restore the smart features of your unit.
