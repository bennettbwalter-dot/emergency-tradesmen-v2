**Trust is everything in an emergency. Inviting a stranger into your home when you are vulnerable requires confidence that they are who they say they are.**

At **Emergency Tradesmen**, we don't just list anyone with a van. We employ a rigorous 6-step verification process to ensure every tradesperson on our platform is qualified, insured, and legitimate. Here is exactly how we vet our network.

---

## 1. Identity & Company Checks (Level 1)
Before a tradesperson can join, we verify their legal identity.
*   **Photo ID:** Passport or Driving Licence upload.
*   **Company Registration:** We check Companies House (UK) or State Business Registries (US) to ensure the LTD/LLC is active and solvent.
*   **Address Verification:** Utility bill proof of trading address. We do not accept PO Boxes.

---

## 2. Qualification Verification (Level 2)
Skills matter. A handyman cannot fix a boiler.
*   **Gas Safe Register (UK):** We check their licence number against the Gas Safe database in real-time. If it's expired, they are suspended immediately.
*   **NICEIC / NAPIT (Electrical):** We verify their "Competent Person Scheme" membership for Part P compliance.
*   **City & Guilds / NVQ:** We request certificates for standard plumbing and carpentry trades.

---

## 3. Public Liability Insurance (Level 3)
Accidents happen. If a plumber floods your home, who pays?
*   **The Check:** We require proof of valid Public Liability Insurance (Minimum £2 Million cover).
*   **The Purpose:** This protects you, the homeowner, from bearing the cost of accidental damage caused during the repair.

---

## 4. Criminal Record Checks (Level 4)
Safety is paramount.
*   **DBS Check (UK):** We encourage Enhanced DBS checks for locksmiths and trades entering homes with vulnerable people.
*   **Background Screen:** We screen for major red flags.

---

## 5. The "Real Work" Audit (Level 5)
Paperwork is one thing; quality is another.
*   **Portfolio Review:** We review photos of past work.
*   **Customer Feedback:** We monitor reviews on Trustpilot, Google, and our internal system.
*   **Three Strikes Rule:** If a tradesperson receives 3 substantiated complaints about quality or conduct (e.g., ghosting, rudeness, overcharging), they are permanently removed from the platform.

---

## 6. Price Transparency Promise
We fight against "Cowboy Pricing." 
*   **No Hidden Fees:** Our partners agree to a transparent pricing structure.
*   **Quote Before Work:** You must agree to the price *before* tools are picked up.
*   **VAT Invoices:** All trades must provide a proper tax invoice.

---

Our badge isn't just a logo; it's a shield. When you use **Emergency Tradesmen**, you are hiring a professional who has passed strict vetting to earn their place. **Safety. Quality. Accountability.** That is our promise.
