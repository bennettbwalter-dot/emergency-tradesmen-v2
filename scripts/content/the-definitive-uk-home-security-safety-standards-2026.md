    <h2>4. Carbon Monoxide & Life Safety: BS EN 50291</h2>
    <p>In 2026, the UK government has effectively made integrated CO monitoring a mandatory standard for all habitable rooms with a fuel-burning appliance. The <strong>BS EN 50291</strong> standard ensures that your alarm can communicate with your smartphone and, in many cases, automatically shut down your boiler if levels exceed safety thresholds.</p>
    <div class="split-dos-donts">
        <div class="dos-column">
            <h3>DO</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Ensure all your alarms are "Interconnected"—when one sounds, they all sound.</li>
                <li>Check the "Expiry Date" on your CO detector. 2026 sensors have a 10-year lifespan.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
        <div class="donts-column">
            <h3>DON'T</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Place CO alarms too close to corners—the "Dead Air" zone can delay detection by minutes.</li>
                <li>Rely on a single battery-only alarm if your property has more than two storeys.</li>
            </ul>
