**Water leaks wait for no one. A small drip today is a collapsed ceiling tomorrow. Knowing the "Red Flag" signs of a plumbing emergency can save you thousands in restoration costs.**

Some plumbing issues are annoying (a dripping tap). Others are catastrophic (a burst pipe). This guide helps you identify the 5 critical signs that mean you need to call a 24-hour emergency plumber immediately.

---

## 1. No Water Pressure (Total Loss)
If you turn on the tap and get nothing, or just a dribble:
*   **The Cause:** It could be a burst service pipe in the street, a frozen main (in winter), or a failure of the PRV (Pressure Reducing Valve).
*   **The Risk:** If the break is underground, water is eroding the soil under your driveway or foundation.
*   **Action:** Call a plumber to trace the leak. If it's the water company's fault, they fix it. If it's on your property, you need a pro.

---

## 2. The Smell of Sewage (Inside)
*   **The Cause:** A dry P-trap, a broken wax ring under the toilet, or a blocked soil stack.
*   **The Risk:** Dangerous sewer gas (Hydrogen Sulfide) and potential backup of raw sewage into your home.
*   **Action:** Do not pour chemicals down the drain. Call a plumber to inspect the venting system.

---

## 3. Water Stain on the Ceiling
*   **The Cause:** A leak in the bathroom upstairs (shower trap, toilet seal, or supply pipe).
*   **The Risk:** Collaboration. Wet drywall loses its strength and can collapse, bringing the ceiling down on your head.
*   **Action:** Poke a hole in the bulge to drain the water. Turn off the water supply. Call a plumber to find the leak.

---

## 4. Gurgling Drains
*   **The Cause:** Air trapped in the system due to a blockage further down the line. When you flush the toilet, the sink gurgles.
*   **The Risk:** A full sewage backup is imminent. The blockage is moving closer to the house.
*   **Action:** Stop using water. Call a plumber to jet the drains.

---

## 5. Frozen Pipes
*   **The Cause:** Extreme cold weather and lack of insulation.
*   **The Risk:** Water expands when it freezes, splitting copper and plastic pipes. When it thaws, it floods.
*   **Action:** Turn off the stopcock immediately. Do not use a naked flame to thaw it. Call a plumber to safely thaw and lag the pipes.

---

## Summary
Don't wait for the flood. **No Water. Sewage Smell. Wet Ceiling. Gurgling. Frozen Pipes.** These are the 5 signs that demand immediate professional attention. Act fast to save your home.
