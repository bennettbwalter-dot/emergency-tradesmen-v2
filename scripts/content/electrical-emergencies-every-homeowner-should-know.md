**Electricity is the lifeblood of a modern home, but it's also the most dangerous utility. Every year, 20,000 UK homes suffer accidental electrical fires.**

The average homeowner can rewire a plug, but few know how to handle a fuse box sparking. This guide covers the essential signs of electrical failure you must recognize to keep your family safe.

---

## 1. Burning Plastic Smell (Fishy Odor)
Have you ever walked into a room and smelled rotten fish or burning plastic?
*   **The Cause:** Overheating components. Specifically, Urea Formaldehyde (the plastic used in switches and sockets) releases a distinct "fishy" smell when it melts.
*   **The Danger:** This is an **Active Fire Risk.** The plastic is melting because the wire connections are loose, causing "Arcing" (micro-sparks) which generate intense heat.
*   **Immediate Action:**
    1.  Turn off the main power at the fuse box.
    2.  Identify the room.
    3.  Call an electrician immediately. Do not ignore it.

---

## 2. Flickering Lights (Not the Bulb)
If your lights dim every time the fridge kicks on, you have a problem.
*   **The Cause:** Loose Neutral Wire.
*   **The Danger:** A loose neutral means the voltage in your house is fluctuating wildy. It can spike from 230V to 400V, frying your TV, computer, and boiler PCB.
*   **The Test:** Turn on a heavy appliance (kettle/microwave). Do the lights dim? If yes, call a pro.

---

## 3. Buzzing Sockets (Arcing)
Sockets should be silent.
*   **The Cause:** Loose wiring inside the terminal. The electricity is jumping across the gap (arcing), creating a buzzing sound.
*   **The Visual Check:** Look for brown scorch marks around the pin holes.
*   **The Fix:** Do not use that socket. Tape over it. Get it replaced.

---

## 4. Tripping RCD (The Safety Switch)
The Residual Current Device (RCD) is the most important device in your home [Electrical Safety First guidance on RCDs](https://www.electricalsafetyfirst.org.uk/guidance/safety-around-the-home/electrical-safety/rccbs-and-rcds/).
*   **What it does:** It monitors the electricity going out vs coming back. If even a tiny amount leaks (e.g., through your body), it cuts the power in 0.04 seconds.
*   **Nuisance Tripping:** If it trips randomly, something is leaking to earth.
*   **Common Culprits:** Outdoor lights (water ingress), old kettles, or a nail through a wire.
*   **The Test:** Press the "T" or "Test" button on the RCD. It should snap OFF instantly. If it sticks or does nothing, **your protection is gone.** Replace it now.

---

## 5. Sparking Fuse Box
If you see or hear sparking from your consumer unit:
*   **Evacuate.** This is a major fire hazard.
*   **Do Not Touch It.** The main incomer (100 Amps) is live even if you turn the switches off.
*   **Call 999.** If there is smoke, it is a fire.
*   **Call UK Power Networks:** They can cut the power at the street.

---

## 6. DIY Electrical Safety Rules
Under Part P of the Building Regulations, you are limited in what you can do [UK Government guidance on Part P](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/electrical-safety-in-the-home).
*   **Allowed:** Replacing a socket front, changing a light fitting (in a non-special location).
*   **Not Allowed:** Adding a new circuit, working in a bathroom/kitchen, changing a fuse box.

### The Golden Rule: Safe Isolation
Never work on electricity without **Proving Dead.**
1.  Turn off the main switch.
2.  Lock it off (put the key in your pocket).
3.  Use a Voltage Tester (not a neon screwdriver) to prove the circuit is dead on a known live source first.

---

## Summary
Electrical emergencies give you warnings. The smell of fish, the buzz of a socket, the flicker of a bulb. **Listen to your house.** Ignoring these signs leads to fires. If in doubt, isolate the circuit and call a registered electrician.
