**Smelling rotten eggs? Hearing a faint hiss in the kitchen? In the UK, gas safety is strictly regulated. A leak isn't just a fire hazard; it is a potential explosion risk that requires an immediate, disciplined response. This is the definitive 2025 UK Guide to Gas Emergencies, the 0800 111 999 "Golden Rule," and your legal rights as a tenant or homeowner.**

---

## 1. Identification: The Mercaptan Additive
Natural gas is inherently odorless, but UK gas networks inject a chemical called **Mercaptan** to ensure even a tiny leak can be smelled by the human nose.
*   **The Smell:** Often described as "Rotten Eggs," sulfur, or old cabbage.
*   **The Sound:** You may hear a whistling or rushing sound near the boiler or the gas meter box.
*   **The Sight:** On external pipes, you might see "frost" forming on a leaking joint (due to the cooling effect of expanding gas) or dead vegetation above a buried pipe.

## 2. The Golden Rule: 0800 111 999
In the United Kingdom, if you smell gas, you have one primary priority: call the **National Gas Emergency Service**. 
*   **The Number:** **0800 111 999**.
*   **The Service:** This line is free, open 24/7, and covers all of England, Scotland, and Wales (operated by networks like Cadent, SGN, NGN, and WWU).
*   **The Speed:** They aim to be at your property within 1 to 2 hours.
*   **The Cost:** The call and the safety check (up to the meter) are completely free. However, they will only make the property "Safe" (often by turning off and capping the supply). They will NOT carry out repairs to your internal pipes or appliances.

## 3. Immediate Safety Actions (The "No Spark" Protocol)
If you suspect a leak, your home could be in the "explosive range." Any spark—even one too small to see—can trigger an ignition.
*   **❌ DO NOT** turn light switches ON or OFF.
*   **❌ DO NOT** use electric doorbells or garage door openers.
*   **❌ DO NOT** smoke or use naked flames.
*   **❌ DO NOT** use your mobile phone or landline inside the building.
*   **✅ DO** open all windows and doors to ventilate the area.
*   **✅ DO** extinguish all naked flames (candles, etc.) if safe to do so.

## 4. How to Isolate Your Supply: The ECV (Emergency Control Valve)
Every UK gas supply has an **ECV**. Knowing where this is can save your life and your home.

### Finding and Operating the Valve
*   **Location:** Usually in the white meter box outside, but also commonly found under the stairs, in the hallway, or in a kitchen cupboard.
*   **The Handle:** Look for a yellow handle or a metal lever on the pipe entering the meter.
*   **The Action:** Turn the handle 90 degrees. If the handle is "In line" with the pipe, the gas is ON. If it is **"Across" (perpendicular)** to the pipe, the gas is OFF.
*   **Once Off:** Once you have isolated the supply, do NOT turn it back on until a Gas Safe registered engineer has carried out a "Tightness Test."

## 5. Carbon Monoxide (CO) vs. Natural Gas: The UK Law
In the UK, thousands of people are affected by Carbon Monoxide poisoning every year. It is often confused with a gas leak, but the two are very different.

### The Silent Killer (CO)
*   **Nature:** Odorless, colorless, tasteless.
*   **Symptoms:** "Flu without a fever"—headaches, sleepiness, blurred vision, and nausea.
*   **The Law (2022 Updates):** Since October 2022, UK landlords **must** install a Carbon Monoxide alarm in any room used as living accommodation which contains a fixed combustion appliance (including gas boilers and fires). Failure to do so can result in a £5,000 fine.

## 6. Case Study: The "Leaking Hob" in London
*   **The Scenario:** A tenant in a South London flat smelled gas in the kitchen. They called the National Gas Emergency line (0800 111 999).
*   **The Response:** An engineer arrived in 45 minutes. They identified a leak on the flexible hose connecting the gas hob.
*   **The Outcome:** The engineer capped the gas supply to the hob but left the boiler running so the tenant had heating and hot water.
*   **The Fix:** The tenant called a **Gas Safe Registered** engineer the next day to replace the hose and reissue a safety certificate (Cost: £120).

## 8. The 12-Month UK Gas Safety Calendar
*   **September:** Book your annual boiler service before the winter rush. This is when leaks are most often discovered.
*   **October:** Test your CO alarms. Replace batteries if they aren't the "10-year sealed" type.
*   **December:** In extreme cold, ensure your external condensate pipe hasn't frozen (which can cause a boiler lockout that mimics a gas fail).
*   **May:** Check your gas meter box door. If it is broken or missing, the meter is exposed to the elements and can corrode. Replacement doors cost about £20 on Amazon.

## 9. Specialized UK Gas Glossary
1.  **ECV:** Emergency Control Valve (The main shut-off).
2.  **ECV Handle:** The yellow lever.
3.  **Gas Safe Register:** The official list of gas engineers (replaced CORGI in 2009).
4.  **HSE:** Health and Safety Executive (Relevant for workplace and landlord gas safety).
5.  **Landlord Gas Safety Record (CP12):** The legal document proving a rental home is safe.
6.  **LPG (Liquid Petroleum Gas):** Bottled gas (Calor) used in rural areas. Heavier than air.
7.  **Tightness Test:** A pressure test an engineer performs to check for leaks.
8.  **U6/G4 Meter:** Common residential meter types in the UK.
9.  **Visual Inspection:** Checking for soot, yellow flames, or pilot light issues.

## 10. Master FAQ: Top UK Homeowner Questions
**Q: I am a tenant and I smell gas. Who pays?**
A: **The Landlord.** Under the *Landlord and Tenant Act 1985*, your landlord is responsible for the safety of gas pipes and appliances. If you smell gas, follow the emergency procedure, then inform your landlord.

**Q: Can I smell gas outside in my street?**
A: **Yes.** This is usually a leak on the "Grid" or "Mains." Call the same 0800 111 999 number. They will treat it as a priority emergency.

**Q: Is it illegal to do my own gas work?**
A: **YES.** Under the *Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998*, it is a criminal offense for anyone to work on a gas appliance unless they are Gas Safe Registered. This includes changing a hob or servicing a boiler.
