## 1. The 9-Second Vulnerability: Lock Snapping
Standard Euro-profile cylinders, found on millions of uPVC and composite doors, can be breached in under ten seconds using basic tools.
- **The Threat:** Burglars apply force to the protruding part of the cylinder, snapping it at its weakest point to expose the internal locking mechanism.
- **The Solution:** Upgrade to **TS007 3-star anti-snap cylinders**. These are designed with sacrificial break points that protect the internal cam during an attack.
- **Expert Resource:** Read about the [2026 Anti-Snap Lock Lockdown](/blog/2026-anti-snap-lock-lockdown-gb) to see if your home is at risk.

## 2. The "Hollow Frame" Trap: Structural Security
Even the best lock is useless if the door frame itself is weak. Many modern frames are secured with short screws that barely enter the decorative trim.
- **The Threat:** A single kick can splinter the wood around the strike plate, bypassing the lock entirely.
- **The Solution:** Replace standard strike plate screws with **3-inch hardened steel structural anchors** that go directly into the wall studs.
- **Expert Resource:** See our [Structural Door Security Guide](/blog/hollow-frame-trap-structural-door-security) for 7 essential upgrades.

## 3. High-Security Deadbolt Upgrades (US)
In the US, the deadbolt is the primary line of defense. However, "Grade 3" locks found in many hardware stores often fail under physical force.
- **Action:** Ensure your deadbolts are **ANSI Grade 1** certified. Combine this with a reinforced strike box to prevent localized structural failure.
- **Expert Resource:** Consult our [2026 High-Security Deadbolt Upgrade Guide](/blog/2026-high-security-deadbolt-upgrade-us).

## 4. Emergency Glazing and Board-Up Services
A smashed window isn't just a draft issue; it's a total security failure.
- **Immediate Action:** If your window is smashed due to storm damage or a break-in, use a professional board-up service to secure the area until a permanent repair can be made.
