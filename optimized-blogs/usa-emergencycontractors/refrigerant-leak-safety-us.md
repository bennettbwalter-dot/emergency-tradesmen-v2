            <li><strong>Frozen Coils (Oddly):</strong> A slow leak causes the remaining refrigerant to drop in pressure and freeze the indoor coil. If you see ice on your indoor unit in 2026, you have a leak.</li>
            <li><strong>Oil Streaks:</strong> Refrigerant oil (POE) is clear and viscous. If you see a "wet" spot on your ductwork or at line-set connections, that's a 2026 leak point.</li>
        </ul>
