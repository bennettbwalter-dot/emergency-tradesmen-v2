            <li><strong>Automated Drip:</strong> Some 2026 smart fixtures can be triggered to "Drip" slightly through the app, maintaining water movement to prevent ice plugs.</li>
        </ul>

If your furnace fails while you're in Florida, your smart water system will alert you that your pipes are freezing, giving you hours to call an emergency contractor before the structural flood begins.</p>
