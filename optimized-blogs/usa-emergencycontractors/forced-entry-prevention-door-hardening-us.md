    <h2>6. Hinge Security: Non-Removable Pins and Safety Studs</h2>
    <div class="capsule-box">
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Out-Swing Risk:</strong> If your door opens outward, your hinge pins are on the outside. A thief can tap them out with a nail and lift your door off its hinges.</li>
            <li><strong>NRP (Non-Removable Pin):</strong> 2026 standards suggest NRP hinges for all out-swinging exterior doors. A hidden set-screw prevents the pin from being pulled.</li>
            <li><strong>Safety Studs:</strong> These are "Internal Pins" that lock the two halves of the hinge together even if the main pin is removed.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
