    <div class="capsule-box">
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Rear Extensions:</strong> Standard PD allows 3m for semi-detached and 4m for detached houses without full planning applications.</li>
            <li><strong>Loft Conversions:</strong> You can add up to 40 cubic metres (terraced) or 50 cubic metres (detached) of space under PD, provided you stay within height limits.</li>
            <li><strong>Porches:</strong> Small additions under 3 square metres are usually exempt, provided they aren't too close to the highway.</li>
        </ul>

    <p>Permitted Development (PD) rights are a homeowner's secret weapon in 2026. They allow for "Rapid Upscaling." However, 2026 updates mean that even PD work must be strictly "Building Control Compliant." You may not need a planning officer, but you absolutely need a building inspector to sign off on your foundations, drainage, and structural beams.</p>
