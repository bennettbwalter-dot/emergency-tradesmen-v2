    <div class="capsule-box">
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Energy Density vs. Safety:</strong> Older NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt) batteries were lighter but prone to "Thermal Runaway" if damaged.</li>
            <li><strong>2026 LFP Mandate:</strong> Most UK insurers now explicitly prefer or require LFP-based indoor storage for residential properties.</li>
            <li><strong>Cycle Life:</strong> An LFP unit like the EcoFlow Delta Pro 3 provides 3,500-6,000 cycles before hitting 80% capacity—roughly 10-15 years of daily "Peak Shaving" use.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
    <p>In 2026, we only recommend LFP-based units for permanent home residence. The chemistry is inherently safer because it doesn't release oxygen when it fails, preventing the "un-extinguishable fires" associated with older electric vehicle batteries. We provide 2026 "Chemistry Audits" to help you phase out legacy Lithium-Ion units that may no longer be compliant with your home's fire-safety plan.</p>

    <div class="capsule-box">

            <li><strong>AVR (Automatic Voltage Regulation):</strong> Modern units filter "Harmonic Noise" from the UK grid, providing cleaner power than what comes out of your wall socket.</li>
        </ul>

    <h2>4. Floating Neutrals & UK Earthing: The Safety Gap in Off-Grid Power</h2>
    <div class="capsule-box">
        <ul>
            <li><strong>The Technical Issue:</strong> Standard UK mains power has a neutral wire bonded to earth at the substation. Portable units often have a "Floating Neutral" for safety in camping scenarios.</li>
            <li><strong>The UK 3-Pin Danger:</strong> If you use a floating neutral unit to power a whole-home circuit, your RCD (safety switch) in the consumer unit may not trip during a fault.</li>
            <li><strong>The 2026 Solution:</strong> Specialist "N-PE Bonding Plugs" are now available for 2026 home use, but they MUST be installed according to IET guidelines to avoid creating a new shock hazard.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
    <p>Earthing is the most misunderstood area of portable power. In 2026, "Island Mode" safety is paramount. We provide professional "Earthing Audits" for UK homeowners who want to integrate portable batteries into their permanent home wiring. Don't assume "Plug and Play" means "Safe and Grounded." Let our 2026 specialists verify your loop impedance.</p>

    <div class="capsule-box">

            <li><strong>Location Audit:</strong> We recommend keeping your "Resilience Cube" in a temperature-controlled utility room or hallway for optimal 2026 performance.</li>
            <li><strong>The Heat Risk:</strong> Conversely, keep units away from radiators; excessive heat is the #1 cause of 2026 LFP "De-Rating."</li>
