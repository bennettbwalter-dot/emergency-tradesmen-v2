  • 97% Uptime Mandate: Under the **NEVI Formula Program**, federally funded US chargers must maintain a 97% uptime. For home chargers, 40% experience a hardware or software fault within the first 3 years.

2. Common Points of Failure in US EVSE

  • NEMA 14-50 Failure: Most US Level 2 failures occur at the plug-in point. High-resistance connections can lead to melting or "Arcing" at the outlet, a severe fire risk.
  • Logic Board Failures: 2026 units rely on **OCPP 2.0.1** for smart functionality. Surges or firmware corruption can "brick" the charger, necessitating a professional logic board swap.
