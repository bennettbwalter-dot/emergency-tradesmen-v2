    <div class="capsule-box">

            <li><strong>A3 Flammability:</strong> Unlike R-32, R-290 is highly flammable. This is why it is almost exclusively used in Monobloc units located *outside* the house.</li>
            <li><strong>Safe Separation:</strong> 2026 MCS standards require a minimum distance between an R-290 unit and any basement window or drain to prevent gas ingress during a leak.</li>

    <h2>5. Identifying a Leak: The 2026 Sensory Checklist</h2>
    <div class="capsule-box">

            <li><strong>The Smell:</strong> Most 2026 refrigerants are odorless, but the escaping POE oil can have a faint, ether-like or "Chemical" scent.</li>
            <li><strong>Performance Drop:</strong> If your 2026 heat pump is running 24/7 but the radiators are barely warm, the "Charge" is low.</li>
        </ul>
